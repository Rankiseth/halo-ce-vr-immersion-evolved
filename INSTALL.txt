Halo CE VR - Immersion Evolved v0.1.0-alpha.1 (R1.37)
Win32 update installation

REQUIRES
- Original Halo PC retail patched to 1.10 (01.00.10.0621).
- An existing working HaloCEVR installation with its 32-bit openvr_api.dll.
- SteamVR and tracked controllers.
- Microsoft Visual C++ v14 Redistributable, x86 edition:
  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist

INSTALL
1. Close Halo. Back up your working game folder, including VR/config.txt.
2. Extract the update ZIP to a temporary folder.
3. Copy all extracted contents into the folder containing halo.exe.
   Replace the files included in the update and merge the VR folders.
4. Keep your existing openvr_api.dll and VR/config.txt. Neither file is included
   in this update. Do not put d3d9.dll into Windows/System32 or SysWOW64.
5. Start SteamVR and launch Halo using your existing working setup.
6. Hold X, then press RT to open the VR menu; in the Ghost, tap Y.
   About should show 0.1.0-alpha.1 / R1.37.
7. Under Comfort, choose Sitting (default) or Standing. Player height is in cm
   and affects Standing only. At 180 cm, the belt is 60 cm below the headset.
   Select Save to retain your settings.

MAGNUM
With Manual Reload enabled, the slide locks fully back when the magazine is
empty. Eject, insert a fresh magazine, then complete the slide pull/release to
chamber it. Tactical reload behavior is retained.

ROLLBACK
Close Halo and restore the complete working folder backup, including its DLL,
VR assets, and configuration.

ALPHA SCOPE
The maintainer confirmed R1.37 in-headset, including the Magnum empty-slide
lock and Sitting/Standing settings. Custom Edition, SPV3, multiplayer, and
individual edge cases are not validated for this alpha. MCC is not supported.
This is an update package, not a complete Halo or HaloCEVR installer.
